About

Brief Bio

I am an undergraduate student at KAIST, with interests in mathematics, statistics, and machine learning. I graduated from the Korea Science Academy of KAIST.

My academic interests center on understanding machine learning from mathematical and statistical perspectives, especially Bayesian inference and uncertainty quantification. I will join the Statistical Inference and Machine Learning (SIML) Lab as a winter research intern after the Fall 2026 semester.

Research Interests

  • Uncertainty quantification in machine learning
  • Mathematical foundations of machine learning
